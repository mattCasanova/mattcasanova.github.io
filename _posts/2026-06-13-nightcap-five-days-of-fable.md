It's 6:50 on a Saturday and I'm starting early.

Portland again — same weekend-off energy as [three weeks ago](/2026/05/22/nightcap-both-shifts-off/), when I took the day and drank straight through. A tart beer, a hazy IPA, and somewhere in the lineup a glass of champagne, because apparently that's the kind of nightcap this is. No occasion. The champagne *is* the occasion.

Small updates this week. But the one that counts: **the side projects are awake again.** After [a month of them frozen](/2026/06/06/nightcap-not-out-of-the-rain-yet/) while the day job ate the calendar, the [thaw the Night Auditor promised](/2026/06/07/the-night-auditor/) actually showed up. Still nothing on Flux or LiquidMetal2D — they keep waiting — but the apps-and-website column moved, and that's the one I've been missing.

### One more thing: I borrowed a Fable

Here's the part I was genuinely excited about. A few days back — the 9th, I think — I got early access to Anthropic's new model. Fable 5. (Or however the name actually goes; I didn't have it long enough to memorize the branding.)

It was supposed to run through the 22nd. It didn't. Somewhere around midweek the access just… went away. I'll leave the *why* alone — I don't actually know it, and the parts I've half-heard aren't mine to guess at. What I can tell you is the model was genuinely good, it was humming, and then almost as fast as it arrived it was gone. Barely five days, and now I've got a fable about a model.

And the funny part — I didn't even use it to write code. I used it to *plan.* Which turned out to be exactly the right way to spend it: Fable drew the maps, Fable vanished, and then this weekend I built from those maps with plain old Opus 4.8 — back to the everyday model, the shiny new one nowhere in the room. Fable's gone. The plans it left behind are running in code, shipped by the regular workhorse.

### What actually got done

- **Provisioning, tightened.** Real work on my server and database provisioning scripts — locking down security I'd left looser than I liked. Shipped.
- **Stripe, live.** This one went from a plan to actual code this weekend: Stripe wired up as the payment processor, plus the product/admin setup — a dashboard for defining products, storing them, and selling them off the website. It's going.
- **Mobile, catching up.** The web side is out ahead; I'm dragging the mobile version up to match, with in-app purchases on the Apple and Google sides as the next stop.

So: provisioning hardened, Stripe running, mobile climbing — all of it built off blueprints a model sketched in the few days before it vanished. Not a bad trade. The projects are moving again — real code now, not just plans — and after the month I just had, that's the whole report.

The champagne's going flat and the hazy's better cold, so I'll wrap.

See you, space cowboy.
