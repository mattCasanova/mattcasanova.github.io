**Day job.** All of it. Not going to say more than I already [said](/2026/05/09/nightcap-still-doing-it/).

**Flux.** Zero.

Three projects, three zeros. Capacity is finite and right now every minute of it is funneled into one column. That's the deal I [signed up for](/2026/05/06/bad-advice-prove-them-wrong/), so I'm not going to complain about it.

### One more thing

Layoffs landed Wednesday. I [called them](/2026/05/06/bad-advice-prove-them-wrong/) two weeks ago — *"another big layoff is coming in a few weeks."* That was the one.

I wasn't on the list.

Was it the weekends? I genuinely don't know. Probably not on its own — there are a lot of inputs that go into a list like that and most of them aren't visible from where I sit. But I choose to believe the weekends didn't hurt. The cost of being wrong about that is one Saturday. The cost of being right and not showing up was a much bigger number.

So today I took off. Probably the first full day off in four weeks. Couch, drink, no laptop within arm's reach. The night shift is off too — drinking straight through to tomorrow.

### Something else

Confession: I have barely touched my personal Claude account in two weeks. Still using AI constantly — Meta's internal one all day, plus the various others we're testing — but the personal Opus tab has been cold.

Which is part of why I'm only now noticing something has gone sideways.

When [Opus 4.7 dropped](/2026/04/17/nightcap-fifteen-posts-deep/), it was great. I [blogged through Vegas](/2026/04/19/five-year-particle-system/) on it. [Particle systems](/2026/04/19/five-year-particle-system/), [render passes](/2026/04/19/render-pass-strikes-back/), [persistence](/2026/04/22/patience-then-persistence/) — that whole weekend was 4.7 doing real work. Best model I'd ever used.

Joke I made to a friend this week: *we lived in the golden age of AI, and it lasted about three and a half months.*

Right now, 4.7 with the 1M context and extra-high effort is not doing things it was doing a month ago. Basic things. I'll point at a chat in the sidebar and say *read this*; it says it did, then answers like it didn't. One-shots take three tries. Something shifted.

I can't tell where. Could be Anthropic quietly throttling. Could be company-level limits on whatever plan I'm bumping into. Enough chatter on Twitter that I don't think it's just me — but from the outside, you can't see which knob got turned.

So I'm going to do something about it. A Mac Studio is on its way. Plan is to run open-source weights locally and see how close to *90% of Claude* I can get on a machine with no meter on it. Not training a model — just running someone else's, and seeing what the daily-driver loop feels like with no quota and no remote degradation.

Could be a wash. Could save real money. Worth knowing the floor either way.
